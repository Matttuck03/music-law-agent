C4. What must our takedown system include on day one?

  • Rows that frame it: registered agent and renewal [M-0349, M-0354]; notice elements [M-0350, M-0351]; counter notice and put-back timing [M-0355, M-0356]; repeat infringer policy [M-0357, M-0376]; liability for knowingly false notices [M-0359].

C5. When does "sharing with friends" become a public performance or distribution?

  • Rows that frame it: the public performance right [M-0106]; the reproduction right [M-0108].
  • Gap: the base has no row on the meaning of "publicly". Do not label any sharing feature private until this is answered.

C6. When a user streams a live mix through us, who performs the works?

  • Rows that frame it: performance rights [M-0106, M-0107]; a licensed service's statement that the service must clear both rights (captured 2026-11-13) [M-0586, M-0590]; video is outside 114 [M-0112].

C7. What does a club or venue licence actually cover, and does any of it carry over online?

  • Rows that frame it: performance is a separate right from reproduction (analysis row) [M-0183]; the consent decrees limit ASCAP to non-exclusive public performance rights [M-0256].
  • Gap: no venue licence text is saved.

  2. [M-0113] VERIFIED. Under 17 U.S.C. 114(j)(7), an interactive service is one that lets a member of the public receive on request a transmission of a particular sound recording selected by or for the recipient.

    Source: 17 U.S.C. 114 scope of exclusive rights in sound recordings. Pinpoint: 17 U.S.C. 114(j)(7). Jurisdiction: US-federal. Checked 2026-11-10.

    Quote: "An interactive service is one that enables a member of the public to receive a transmission of a program specially created for the recipient, or on request, a transmission of a particular sound recording"

  3. [M-0585] VERIFIED. A service is not interactive just because users can request recordings for the public at large, unless the channel programming substantially consists of recordings performed within 1 hour of the request or at a designated time.

    Source: 17 U.S.C. 114 scope of exclusive rights in sound recordings. Pinpoint: 17 U.S.C. 114(j)(7). Jurisdiction: US-federal. Checked 2026-11-13.

    Quote: "The ability of individuals to request that particular sound recordings be performed for reception by the public at large"

  4. [M-0580] VERIFIED. Under the statutory licence the transmission may not exceed the sound recording performance complement, and the service may not publish an advance schedule of the specific recordings.

    Source: 17 U.S.C. 114 scope of exclusive rights in sound recordings. Pinpoint: 17 U.S.C. 114(d)(2)(C)(i)-(ii). Jurisdiction: US-federal. Checked 2026-11-13.

    Quote: "the transmitting entity does not cause to be published, or induce or facilitate the publication, by means of an advance program schedule or prior announcement, the titles of the specific sound recordings to be transmitted"

  5. [M-0581] VERIFIED. The sound recording performance complement allows, in any 3-hour period on a channel, no more than 3 different selections from any one phonorecord (no more than 2 consecutively), or 4 different selections by the same featured artist or from any set or compilation of phonorecords (no more than 3 consecutively).

    Source: 17 U.S.C. 114 scope of exclusive rights in sound recordings. Pinpoint: 17 U.S.C. 114(j)(13). Jurisdiction: US-federal. Checked 2026-11-13.

    Quote: "3 different selections of sound recordings from any one phonorecord lawfully distributed for public performance or sale in the United States, if no more than 2 such selections are transmitted consecutively"

  6. [M-0582] VERIFIED. The statutory licence requires the service to take no affirmative steps to cause or induce the recipient to make a phonorecord, so a download button on mixes is outside it.

    Source: 17 U.S.C. 114 scope of exclusive rights in sound recordings. Pinpoint: 17 U.S.C. 114(d)(2)(C)(vi). Jurisdiction: US-federal. Checked 2026-11-13.

    Quote: "the transmitting entity takes no affirmative steps to cause or induce the making of a phonorecord by the transmission recipient"

  7. [M-0583] VERIFIED. The statutory licence requires the transmission to be made from a phonorecord lawfully made under the copyright owner's authority.

    Source: 17 U.S.C. 114 scope of exclusive rights in sound recordings. Pinpoint: 17 U.S.C. 114(d)(2)(C)(vii). Jurisdiction: US-federal. Checked 2026-11-13.
