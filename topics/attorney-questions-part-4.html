  3. [M-0591] VERIFIED. Mixcloud says that on a platform that has not cleared the music, rights holders can have a mix taken down and the mix muted.

    Source: Mixcloud blog: how to live stream music without copyright takedown issues (Mixcloud's own blog). Pinpoint: Mixcloud blog 2024-03-28, 'What is a DMCA takedown'. Jurisdiction: platform-terms. Checked 2026-11-13.

    Quote: "the copyright holders can ask for it to be taken down, and your mixes, shows or audio will be muted"

  4. [M-0108] VERIFIED. Both musical works and sound recordings carry the reproduction right (copies or phonorecords).

    Source: 17 U.S.C. 106 exclusive rights in copyrighted works. Pinpoint: 17 U.S.C. 106(1). Jurisdiction: US-federal. Checked 2026-11-10.

    Quote: "to reproduce the copyrighted work in copies or phonorecords;"

  5. [M-0542] VERIFIED. The compulsory licence is for making and distributing phonorecords of a nondramatic musical work (the composition); it is not a licence of any sound recording.

    Source: 17 U.S.C. 115 compulsory license for making and distributing phonorecords. Pinpoint: 17 U.S.C. 115(a)(1)(A)-(B). Jurisdiction: US-federal. Checked 2026-11-13.

    Quote: "obtain a compulsory license to make and distribute phonorecords of a nondramatic musical work"

  6. [M-0540] VERIFIED. A compulsory licence under 115 includes the privilege of an arrangement only to the extent necessary to conform the work to the style of the performance; it may not change the basic melody or fundamental character.

    Source: 17 U.S.C. 115 compulsory license for making and distributing phonorecords. Pinpoint: 17 U.S.C. 115(a)(2). Jurisdiction: US-federal. Checked 2026-11-13.

    Quote: "the arrangement shall not change the basic melody or fundamental character of the work"

  7. [M-0544] VERIFIED. The Office states a phonorecord does not include sounds accompanying a motion picture or other audiovisual work, so the compulsory licence does not cover putting a song in video.

    Source: Copyright Office Circular 73, Compulsory license for making and distributing phonorecords. Pinpoint: Circular 73. Jurisdiction: US-federal. Checked 2026-11-13.

    Quote: "A phonorecord does not include sounds accompanying a motion picture or other audiovisual work"

  8. [M-0588] VERIFIED. Mixcloud says downloading music needs an additional licensed right, the mechanical right.

    Source: Mixcloud blog: how to live stream music without copyright takedown issues (Mixcloud's own blog). Pinpoint: Mixcloud blog 2024-03-28. Jurisdiction: platform-terms. Checked 2026-11-13.

    Quote: "Downloading music online requires an additional licensed right, the mechanical right."

  9. [M-0023] VERIFIED. Under the Music Modernization Act, a digital music provider can obtain a blanket mechanical license through the mechanical licensing collective.

    Source: 17 U.S.C. 115 compulsory license for making and distributing phonorecords. Pinpoint: 17 U.S.C. 115(d)(1)(A). Jurisdiction: US-federal. Checked 2026-11-10.

    Quote: "A digital music provider that qualifies for a compulsory license under subsection (a) may, by complying with the terms and conditions of this subsection, obtain a blanket license from copyright owners through the mechanical licensing collective"

  10. [M-0122] VERIFIED. The blanket licence lets a qualifying digital music provider obtain a licence through the MLC for digital phonorecord deliveries.

    Source: 17 U.S.C. 115 compulsory license for making and distributing phonorecords. Pinpoint: 17 U.S.C. 115(d)(1)(A). Jurisdiction: US-federal. Checked 2026-11-10.

    Quote: "A digital music provider that qualifies for a compulsory license under subsection (a) may, by complying with the terms and conditions of this subsection, obtain a blanket license from copyright owners through the mechanical licensing collective"

  11. [M-0125] VERIFIED. The blanket licence covers all musical works (or shares) available for compulsory licensing for covered activities, except as provided in subparagraph (C) (voluntary licences and individual download authority).

    Source: 17 U.S.C. 115 compulsory license for making and distributing phonorecords. Pinpoint: 17 U.S.C. 115(d)(1)(B)(i). Jurisdiction: US-federal. Checked 2026-11-10.

    Quote: "covers all musical works (or shares of such works) available for compulsory licensing under this section for purposes of engaging in covered activities, except as provided in subparagraph (C);"

  12. [M-0545] VERIFIED. First-use rule: the compulsory licence is available only if phonorecords of the work have previously been distributed to the public in the US under the authority of the copyright owner, or, for a digital music provider's digital phonorecord deliveries, the first fixation of the sound recording was made under the musical work owner's authority and the recording owner authorised the deliveries.

    Source: 17 U.S.C. 115 compulsory license for making and distributing phonorecords. Pinpoint: 17 U.S.C. 115(a)(1)(A)(i)-(ii). Jurisdiction: US-federal. Checked 2026-11-13.

    Quote: "phonorecords of such musical work have previously been distributed to the public in the United States under the authority of the copyright owner of the work"

  13. [M-0546] VERIFIED. For non-digital phonorecords, notice of intention must be served on the owner before, or within 30 days after, making and before distributing any phonorecords; failure forecloses the compulsory licence and, absent a voluntary licence, makes the making and distribution actionable as infringement.

    Source: 17 U.S.C. 115 compulsory license for making and distributing phonorecords. Pinpoint: 17 U.S.C. 115(b)(1) and (b)(4)(A). Jurisdiction: US-federal. Checked 2026-11-13.

    Quote: "the failure to serve or file the notice of intention required by paragraph (1) forecloses the possibility of a compulsory license"

  14. [M-0541] VERIFIED. An arrangement made under the compulsory licence is not protected as a derivative work except with the express consent of the composition owner.

    Source: 17 U.S.C. 115 compulsory license for making and distributing phonorecords. Pinpoint: 17 U.S.C. 115(a)(2). Jurisdiction: US-federal. Checked 2026-11-13.

    Quote: "shall not be subject to protection as a derivative work under this title, except with the express consent of the copyright owner"

  15. [M-0543] VERIFIED. The Office says that with the compulsory licence you cannot copy a sound recording publicly distributed in phonorecords without permission, and cannot change the basic melody or claim your arrangement as a derivative work.

    Source: Copyright Office Circular 73, Compulsory license for making and distributing phonorecords. Pinpoint: Circular 73. Jurisdiction: US-federal. Checked 2026-11-13.

    Quote: "Change the basic melody or fundamental character of the work in the arrangement"

  16. [M-0347] VERIFIED. The provider must not receive a financial benefit directly attributable to infringement where it has the right and ability to control it.

    Source: 17 U.S.C. 512 limitations on liability relating to material online. Pinpoint: 17 U.S.C. 512(c)(1)(B). Jurisdiction: US-federal. Checked 2026-11-10.

    Quote: "does not receive a financial benefit directly attributable to the infringing activity, in a case in which the service provider has the right and ability to control such activity"

  17. [M-0515] VERIFIED. Ninth Circuit (Mavrix v. LiveJournal, 2017): the 512(c) financial benefit test is a common-sense, fact-based inquiry, and the benefit need not be substantial or a large proportion of revenue.

    Source: Mavrix Photographs v. LiveJournal, 873 F.3d 1045 (9th Cir. 2017). Pinpoint: 512(c)(1)(B) discussion. Jurisdiction: case-law. Checked 2026-11-10.

    Quote: "The financial benefit need not be substantial or a large proportion of the service provider’s revenue."

  18. [M-0252] VERIFIED. Analysis: after Cox v. Sony (U.S. 2026) a service provider is contributorily liable for a user's infringement only if it induced the infringement or the service is tailored to it; knowing that some users infringe is not enough. Vicarious liability is a separate theory that the Court did not decide (cert limited to contributory).

    Source: Cox Communications, Inc. v. Sony Music Entertainment, No. 24-171, 607 U.S. ___ (Mar. 25, 2026). Pinpoint: 607 U.S. ___ (2026), syllabus. Jurisdiction: analysis. Checked 2026-11-10.

    Quote: "Held: The provider of a service is contributorily liable for a user’s infringement only if it intended that the provided service be used for infringement,"

  19. [M-0549] VERIFIED. Campbell: a commercial nature is only one element of factor one; no presumption that every commercial use is unfair.

    Source: Campbell v. Acuff-Rose Music, Inc., 510 U.S. 569 (1994) (U.S. Reports via Library of Congress). Pinpoint: 510 U.S. 569, syllabus. Jurisdiction: case-law. Checked 2026-11-13.

    Quote: "The statute makes clear that a work's commercial nature is only one element of the first factor enquiry into its purpose and character"

  20. [M-0553] VERIFIED. Warhol: commercial nature is relevant but not dispositive, and is weighed against the degree to which the use has a further purpose or different character.

    Source: Andy Warhol Foundation for the Visual Arts, Inc. v. Goldsmith, 598 U.S. 508 (2023). Pinpoint: 598 U.S. 508, syllabus. Jurisdiction: case-law. Checked 2026-11-13.

    Quote: "The commercial nature of a use is relevant, but not dispositive"

  21. [M-0215] VERIFIED. Statutory damages range from $750 to $30,000 per work, as the court considers just, at the owner's election.

    Source: 17 U.S.C. 504 remedies, damages and profits. Pinpoint: 17 U.S.C. 504(c)(1). Jurisdiction: US-federal. Checked 2026-11-10.

    Quote: "in a sum of not less than $750 or more than $30,000 as the court considers just."

  22. [M-0216] VERIFIED. Statutory damages are assessed per work for all infringements involved in the action; the owner may elect them any time before final judgment.

    Source: 17 U.S.C. 504 remedies, damages and profits. Pinpoint: 17 U.S.C. 504(c)(1). Jurisdiction: US-federal. Checked 2026-11-10.

    Quote: "an award of statutory damages for all infringements involved in the action, with respect to any one"

  23. [M-0217] VERIFIED. Willful infringement raises the statutory maximum to $150,000 per work.

    Source: 17 U.S.C. 504 remedies, damages and profits. Pinpoint: 17 U.S.C. 504(c)(2). Jurisdiction: US-federal. Checked 2026-11-10.

    Quote: "the court in its discretion may increase the award of statutory damages to a sum of not more than $150,000."

  24. [M-0214] VERIFIED. No statutory damages or fees for infringement of a published work that commenced before its registration, unless registered within three months of publication.

    Source: 17 U.S.C. 412 registration as prerequisite to certain remedies. Pinpoint: 17 U.S.C. 412(2). Jurisdiction: US-federal. Checked 2026-11-10.

    Quote: "any infringement of copyright commenced after first publication of the work and before the effective date of its registration, unless such registration is made within three months after the first publication of the work."

  25. [M-0218] VERIFIED. The civil statute of limitations is three years after the claim accrued.

    Source: 17 U.S.C. 507 limitations on actions. Pinpoint: 17 U.S.C. 507(b). Jurisdiction: US-federal. Checked 2026-11-10.

    Quote: "No civil action shall be maintained under the provisions of this title unless it is commenced within three years after the claim accrued."
