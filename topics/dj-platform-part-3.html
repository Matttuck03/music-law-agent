DJ-mix platform playbook

Part 3 of 7: What needs deals. Law last checked 2026-11-13.

Parts: 1 2 3 4 5 6 7

What needs deals

  • Public sharing of saved mixes that contain commercial tracks.
  • Needs: licences for the recordings and for the songs [M-0301, M-0106, M-0107]. The 114 statutory licence does not fit saved or on-demand mixes [M-0115, M-0578, M-0579].
  • How others do it: one licensed service says it has direct deals with labels, and another says it pays rights holders in each mix directly (company statements, captured 2026-11-13) [M-0589, M-0593].
  • Live mixing of commercial tracks to an audience. Both the song and the recording are performed for every track [M-0106, M-0107]. One licensed service says the service must clear both (company blog, captured 2026-11-13) [M-0586].
  • Downloads and export of mixes that contain commercial tracks. A download is a copy and a distribution of every work in the mix [M-0108]. One licensed service says downloads need an extra licensed right (company blog, captured 2026-11-13) [M-0588].
  • Video of mixes. Video is outside the 114 licence [M-0112] and outside the compulsory mechanical licence [M-0544]. Sync is licensed directly [M-0140].
  • An EU launch with public sharing. The platform itself needs authorisation for user uploads, or must meet the best efforts tests [M-0387, M-0389].
  • The partner route: publish mixes only inside a service that already holds the licences. This still needs a contract with that service. The base has only such services' public statements, not their contract terms (gap) [M-0586, M-0590].

Claims cited on this page

Each claim gives its status, source, pinpoint, jurisdiction, the date it was checked and a verbatim quote from the source.

  1. [M-0301] VERIFIED. An interactive service may not publicly perform a sound recording unless a licence has been granted for the public performance of any copyrighted musical work contained in it.

    Source: 17 U.S.C. 114 scope of exclusive rights in sound recordings. Pinpoint: 17 U.S.C. 114(d)(3)(C). Jurisdiction: US-federal. Checked 2026-11-10.

    Quote: "an interactive service may not publicly perform a sound recording unless a license has been granted for the public performance of any copyrighted musical work contained in the sound recording"

  2. [M-0106] VERIFIED. Musical works carry an exclusive right of public performance.

    Source: 17 U.S.C. 106 exclusive rights in copyrighted works. Pinpoint: 17 U.S.C. 106(4). Jurisdiction: US-federal. Checked 2026-11-10.

    Quote: "in the case of literary, musical, dramatic, and choreographic works, pantomimes, and motion pictures and other audiovisual works, to perform the copyrighted work publicly;"

  3. [M-0107] VERIFIED. Sound recordings carry a public performance right only by digital audio transmission.

    Source: 17 U.S.C. 106 exclusive rights in copyrighted works. Pinpoint: 17 U.S.C. 106(6). Jurisdiction: US-federal. Checked 2026-11-10.

    Quote: "in the case of sound recordings, to perform the copyrighted work publicly by means of a digital audio transmission."

  4. [M-0115] VERIFIED. The statutory licence requires that the transmission not be part of an interactive service.

    Source: 17 U.S.C. 114 scope of exclusive rights in sound recordings. Pinpoint: 17 U.S.C. 114(d)(2)(A)(i). Jurisdiction: US-federal. Checked 2026-11-10.

    Quote: "the transmission is not part of an interactive service;"

  5. [M-0578] VERIFIED. The 114(d)(2) statutory licence for eligible nonsubscription transmissions requires that the transmission is not part of an archived program of less than 5 hours.

    Source: 17 U.S.C. 114 scope of exclusive rights in sound recordings. Pinpoint: 17 U.S.C. 114(d)(2)(C)(iii)(I). Jurisdiction: US-federal. Checked 2026-11-13.

    Quote: "is not part of an archived program of less than 5 hours duration"

  6. [M-0579] VERIFIED. Under the 114 statutory licence a transmission may not be part of an archived program of 5 hours or more that is made available for more than 2 weeks, so a permanent on-demand mix archive does not meet that condition.

    Source: 17 U.S.C. 114 scope of exclusive rights in sound recordings. Pinpoint: 17 U.S.C. 114(d)(2)(C)(iii)(II). Jurisdiction: US-federal. Checked 2026-11-13.

    Quote: "is not part of an archived program of 5 hours or greater in duration that is made available for a period exceeding 2 weeks"

  7. [M-0589] VERIFIED. Mixcloud's live-stream checklist asks whether the platform has direct deals with the record labels whose music is played.

    Source: Mixcloud blog: how to live stream music without copyright takedown issues (Mixcloud's own blog). Pinpoint: Mixcloud blog 2024-03-28, checklist. Jurisdiction: platform-terms. Checked 2026-11-13.

    Quote: "Does it have direct deals with the record labels whose music you play?"

  8. [M-0593] VERIFIED. Apple Music says it works with major and independent labels and pays rights holders within DJ mixes directly.

    Source: Apple Music for Artists: Apple is using Shazam to identify and compensate all DJ mix creators. Pinpoint: Apple Music for Artists notice 2021-11-16. Jurisdiction: platform-terms. Checked 2026-11-13.

    Quote: "Apple Music is directly paying rights holders within these DJ mixes and creating long-term monetary value for all creators involved"

  9. [M-0586] VERIFIED. Mixcloud's own blog says the streaming service must hold licences clearing both the composition and the sound recording of every track in a stream.

    Source: Mixcloud blog: how to live stream music without copyright takedown issues (Mixcloud's own blog). Pinpoint: Mixcloud blog 2024-03-28, 'How can you live stream safely'. Jurisdiction: platform-terms. Checked 2026-11-13.

    Quote: "the streaming service you use needs licences that clear both the musical composition and the sound recording of every track you play"

  10. [M-0108] VERIFIED. Both musical works and sound recordings carry the reproduction right (copies or phonorecords).

    Source: 17 U.S.C. 106 exclusive rights in copyrighted works. Pinpoint: 17 U.S.C. 106(1). Jurisdiction: US-federal. Checked 2026-11-10.

    Quote: "to reproduce the copyrighted work in copies or phonorecords;"

  11. [M-0588] VERIFIED. Mixcloud says downloading music needs an additional licensed right, the mechanical right.

    Source: Mixcloud blog: how to live stream music without copyright takedown issues (Mixcloud's own blog). Pinpoint: Mixcloud blog 2024-03-28. Jurisdiction: platform-terms. Checked 2026-11-13.

    Quote: "Downloading music online requires an additional licensed right, the mechanical right."

  12. [M-0112] VERIFIED. A "digital audio transmission" under 17 U.S.C. 114(j)(5) does not include the transmission of any audiovisual work, so the 114(d)(2) statutory licence for digital audio transmissions does not extend to audiovisual transmission such as a video with music.

    Source: 17 U.S.C. 114 scope of exclusive rights in sound recordings. Pinpoint: 17 U.S.C. 114(j)(5). Jurisdiction: US-federal. Checked 2026-11-10.

    Quote: "This term does not include the transmission of any audiovisual work."

  13. [M-0544] VERIFIED. The Office states a phonorecord does not include sounds accompanying a motion picture or other audiovisual work, so the compulsory licence does not cover putting a song in video.

    Source: Copyright Office Circular 73, Compulsory license for making and distributing phonorecords. Pinpoint: Circular 73. Jurisdiction: US-federal. Checked 2026-11-13.

    Quote: "A phonorecord does not include sounds accompanying a motion picture or other audiovisual work"

  14. [M-0140] VERIFIED. Copyright Office 2015: consumer audiovisual (sync) uses should stay in the free market; no compulsory licence recommended.

    Source: U.S. Copyright Office, Copyright and the Music Marketplace (February 2015). Pinpoint: Copyright and the Music Marketplace (2015), Executive Summary, 'Maintain audiovisual uses in the free market'. Jurisdiction: industry. Checked 2026-11-10.

    Quote: "uses that have traditionally required a synchronization license negotiated in the free market."

  15. [M-0387] VERIFIED. CDSM Art 17(1): an online content-sharing service provider performs an act of communication to the public when it gives access to user uploads, and must get authorisation.

    Source: Directive (EU) 2019/790 on copyright and related rights in the Digital Single Market (CDSM Directive). Pinpoint: Art. 17(1). Jurisdiction: EU. Checked 2026-11-10.

    Quote: "performs an act of communication to the public or an act of making available to the public for the purposes of this Directive when it gives the public access to copyright-protected works"

  16. [M-0389] VERIFIED. Art 17(4)(a): absent authorisation the provider is liable unless it made best efforts to obtain one.

    Source: Directive (EU) 2019/790 on copyright and related rights in the Digital Single Market (CDSM Directive). Pinpoint: Art. 17(4)(a). Jurisdiction: EU. Checked 2026-11-10.

    Quote: "made best efforts to obtain an authorisation"

  17. [M-0590] VERIFIED. Mixcloud describes its live product as a licensed live streaming platform that also archives the stream as a recording.

    Source: Mixcloud blog: how to live stream music without copyright takedown issues (Mixcloud's own blog). Pinpoint: Mixcloud blog 2024-03-28. Jurisdiction: platform-terms. Checked 2026-11-13.

    Quote: "Mixcloud Live is the world's first music-focused, licensed live streaming platform."
