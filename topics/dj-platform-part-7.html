What this page does not settle

  • Whether stem splitting is itself a reproduction. No saved case.
  • Whether a DJ mix is a compilation, a derivative work or both. No saved authority decides [M-0569, M-0521].
  • What a club or venue licence covers. No venue licence text is saved.
  • Live-stream music terms of services other than Mixcloud. Not captured.
  • The contract terms and prices of any licensed mix service. Not public in the saved set.

Claims cited on this page

Each claim gives its status, source, pinpoint, jurisdiction, the date it was checked and a verbatim quote from the source.

  1. [M-0569] VERIFIED. A compilation is a work formed by collecting and assembling preexisting materials selected, coordinated or arranged so the whole is an original work of authorship.

    Source: 17 U.S.C. 101 definitions. Pinpoint: 17 U.S.C. 101 'compilation'. Jurisdiction: US-federal. Checked 2026-11-13.

    Quote: "selected, coordinated, or arranged in such a way that the resulting work as a whole constitutes an original work of authorship"

  2. [M-0521] VERIFIED. A derivative work is one based on preexisting works; the statute's examples include a musical arrangement and a sound recording, and 'any other form in which a work may be recast, transformed, or adapted'.

    Source: 17 U.S.C. 101 definitions. Pinpoint: 17 U.S.C. 101 'derivative work'. Jurisdiction: US-federal. Checked 2026-11-13.

    Quote: "any other form in which a work may be recast, transformed, or adapted"
