M01 Composition and master

Parts: 1 2

Open questions for counsel

  • US federal: for a short video with a commercial track baked into the audio, do you read the section 101 definitions to leave the track's own sound recording copyright intact, and is there case law supporting that (M-0032 is only an inference, unverified)?
  • US federal: does a platform re-encoding a creator's uploaded audio need its own licence, or can it rely on the creator's licence and a terms of service allocation?
  • US federal: is a live stream performing a licensed song a performance by the platform, the creator or both, and which PRO or direct licences must exist?
  • US federal: for a commissioned track used in creator videos, is a master "made for hire" under the audiovisual or compilation category in the current case law (M-0047)?
  • Which counsel should draft the creator terms that require creators to hold both composition and master permissions?
