M04 CRB rates and proceedings (Phonorecords, Web, NSS)

Part 2 of 3: Rules (continued), Gotchas, Open questions for counsel. Law last checked 2026-11-10. Volatility: high.

Parts: 1 2 3

Rules (continued)

  • Web V commercial webcaster rates, 2025 only: $0.0032 per performance (subscription) and $0.0025 per performance (nonsubscription) [M-0154, M-0307]. Minimum fee $1,000 per channel or station per year [M-0155]. Royalties are credited 5 percent to ephemeral recordings and 95 percent to section 114 [M-0156].
  • Voluntary licence agreements may apply in lieu of the Part 380 rates and terms [M-0306].
  • Web VI (2026 to 2030) commenced by Federal Register notice on January 5, 2024 [M-0184]. The Judges adopted four final rules, effective March 10, 2026 [M-0010].
  • Web VI commercial broadcasters: $0.0028 per performance in 2026 rising to $0.0032 in 2030 [M-0159]. Minimum fee $1,100 (2026) to $1,250 (2029 and 2030) [M-0160]. The rule came from a SoundExchange and NAB settlement [M-0162], proposed May 16, 2025 (90 FR 20977) [M-0185].
  • Web VI Educational Media Foundation: $7,125,000 for 2026, rising to about $8.3 million in 2030, one named licensee [M-0163]. Noncommercial educational webcaster minimum fee starts at $800 in 2026 [M-0164].
  • A Commercial Broadcaster is a Commercial Webcaster a material part of whose business is owning and operating FCC licensed terrestrial AM or FM stations. A non broadcaster app is outside that subpart [M-0161].
  • Commercial non broadcaster Web VI rule: not published as of 2026-11-10 in the saved set. The saved sources show only the four settlement rules. That absence rests on a search capture, not rule text (unverified) [M-0182].

New subscription services: NSS V (37 CFR Part 383)

  • NSS V (2026 to 2030) covers digital performances by new subscription services that provide transmissions to residential subscribers as part of a cable or satellite television bundle [M-0166].
  • Stand alone contracts: the 2025 base of $0.0234 per subscriber per month, as adjusted by the annual royalty fee adjustment [M-0165]. Bundled contracts: $0.0390 base, same adjustment [M-0507]. The saved text has placeholders for the 2026 amounts, so the 2026 figure is not stated here.

Gotchas

  • "Statutory rate means I can use any song in my video." No. The rates are for the statutory licences only, and Part 385 is limited to compulsory licence use [M-0138].
  • The 13.1 cent figure is a 2026 rate only. Check the notice for 2027 when it publishes [M-0142, M-0143]. The saved eCFR file also holds an older Phonorecords III copy (9.1 cents); ignore it [M-0139].
  • Web V ($0.0025 and $0.0032) is 2025 only. Do not use it as a 2026 rate [M-0154, M-0307].
  • Web VI is not one rule. Four rules are final for broadcasters, EMF and noncommercial educational webcasters [M-0010]. A commercial non broadcaster rule is not published as of 2026-11-10 (unverified) [M-0182]. Never state a Web VI commercial non broadcaster rate.
  • Phonorecords V is a proposal. A Federal Register search on 2026-11-10 showed no later final rule for docket 25-CRB-0013-PR, so final adoption is unconfirmed [M-0152]. Not law.
  • NSS V 2026 amounts are bracketed in the saved text. Needs source: the December 2025 final rate notice [M-0165].
  • Rate periods end on set dates (Phonorecords IV on December 31, 2027 [M-0148]). Expect a new determination or an adopted settlement for 2028 on.
  • Treating a CRB rate as a cap on a direct licence quote is wrong. Direct deals are priced by agreement [M-0139].

Open questions for counsel

  • US federal: does any statutory licence (115, 114, 112(e)) apply to a short video app that distributes user videos with licensed or unlicensed music, or is every such use a sync plus master direct licence?
  • US federal: if the Judges adopt the Phonorecords V Subpart B settlement, does anything change for an app that is not a phonorecord distributor?
  • US federal: is any platform feature (live streams, audio only streams) a digital audio transmission that could fall under Web VI, and which rate class would apply with no commercial non broadcaster rule published?
  • US federal: is a platform's audio plan, if any, a new subscription service within NSS V, or outside it?
  • US federal: what does the commercial non broadcaster Web VI status mean for exposure today?
