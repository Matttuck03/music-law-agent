M04 CRB rates and proceedings (Phonorecords, Web, NSS)

Part 1 of 3: Bottom line, Rules. Law last checked 2026-11-10. Volatility: high.

Answers questions like: What is the current mechanical rate; Do the CRB webcasting rates cover a platform that streams user mixes; What is Web VI and is there a rate for a mixing app; What is Phonorecords V and is it final; How long do CRB proceedings take and can they be appealed; Can a voluntary licence replace the CRB rate

Parts: 1 2 3

Bottom line

  • US federal: the Copyright Royalty Judges (CRB) set the rates and terms for the statutory licences in 17 U.S.C. 112(e), 114 and 115 [M-0170]. Section 115 is the mechanical licence for the musical work (composition) [M-0148]. Sections 112(e) and 114 are the digital performance and ephemeral licences for the sound recording (master) [M-0158].
  • None of these rates is a price for putting music in a video. Part 385 says it sets rates only where a section 115 compulsory licence is obtained and the exclusive rights are implicated [M-0138]. Sync (composition) and master use (recording) still need direct licences from the rights holders (analysis).
  • Rates move by period. The 2026 mechanical rate is 13.1 cents per work or 2.52 cents per minute, whichever is larger, for 2026 only [M-0142, M-0143]. Web V rates are 2025 only. Web VI is not fully codified for a commercial non broadcaster app (unverified) [M-0182]. Phonorecords V is a proposed settlement, not law.

Rules

Who sets rates, and how (17 U.S.C. 801 to 804)

  • US federal (statute): the Librarian of Congress appoints three full time Copyright Royalty Judges, one of them Chief [M-0169].
  • The Judges determine reasonable terms and rates under sections 112(e), 114, 115, 116, 118, 119 and 1004 [M-0170].
  • The Judges may adopt a settlement among some or all participants. They may decline to bind non parties only if a participant objects and the record shows no reasonable basis [M-0171].
  • Section 115 (Phonorecords) proceedings recur every fifth calendar year, or at other times agreed under 115(c)(3)(B) to (C) [M-0177].
  • Timeline (statute): for section 804(b)(2) proceedings, notice of commencement by January 5 of the year specified [M-0172]. Phonorecords V commenced December 31, 2025 [M-0150]. Petitions to participate are due 30 days after that notice [M-0173]. The voluntary negotiation period is 3 months [M-0174]. The determination is due not later than 11 months after the 21 day settlement conference period ends [M-0175].
  • Appeal goes to the D.C. Circuit within 30 days after Federal Register publication [M-0176].
  • The Copyright Office opened the periodic review of the MLC designation by notification of inquiry on January 30, 2024 (89 FR 5940) [M-0132].
  • Reports of use: preexisting subscription services must deliver them to the designated Collective within 45 days after the close of each month (37 CFR 370.3(b)) [M-0178]. A Collective is an organization designated under a statutory licence by CRB determination [M-0305].

Phonorecords: section 115 mechanical rates (37 CFR Part 385)

  • Part 385 applies only where the exclusive rights of a copyright owner are implicated and a section 115 compulsory licence is obtained [M-0138].
  • Voluntary licence rates and terms apply in lieu of Part 385 [M-0139].
  • Phonorecords IV (regulation): applies January 1, 2023 through December 31, 2027 [M-0148]. A March 18, 2024 correction fixed capitalization and a late fee term [M-0149].
  • Physical phonorecords and permanent downloads, 2026 only: the larger of 13.1 cents per work or 2.52 cents per minute of playing time or fraction [M-0142]. The December 1, 2025 notice applied the cost of living adjustment for January 1 to December 31, 2026 [M-0143]. It used a CPI-U of 324.800 against a November 2022 base of 298.012 [M-0144]. The rate adjusts each year.
  • Ringtones: flat 24 cents per work [M-0145].
  • Streaming and limited downloads (Subpart C, 2023 to 2027): the all in royalty is the greater of a percent of service provider revenue and the TCC prong, with Mixed Service Bundles excepted [M-0147]. The revenue percent runs 15.1 (2023) to 15.35 (2027) [M-0146].
  • Phonorecords V (2028 to 2032): the Judges announced commencement on December 31, 2025 [M-0150]. Petitions to participate were due January 30, 2026 [M-0151].
  • Phonorecords V proposal: on July 10, 2026 the Judges published a proposed partial settlement that would continue the Subpart B rates (physical, permanent downloads, ringtones, music bundles) for 2028 to 2032 with only continuing inflation adjustments to physical and permanent download rates [M-0152]. Comments were due August 10, 2026 [M-0152]. The notice covers Subpart B configurations only [M-0153]. It does not settle streaming (analysis, from M-0153).

Webcasting: sections 114 and 112(e) (37 CFR Parts 380, 383)

  • Web V (regulation): Part 380 codified rates for January 1, 2021 through December 31, 2025. That period has ended [M-0157, M-0158, M-0306].
