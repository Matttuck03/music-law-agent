M06 SoundExchange, 114 and 112

Part 2 of 3: Rules (continued), Gotchas, Open questions for counsel. Law last checked 2026-11-10. Volatility: high.

Parts: 1 2 3

Rules (continued)

  • Web VI commercial broadcasters (settlement between SoundExchange and NAB, adopted as proposed) [M-0162, M-0185]: nonsubscription rate $0.0028 per performance in 2026 rising to $0.0032 in 2030 [M-0159]. Minimum fee $1,100 (2026) to $1,250 (2029, 2030) [M-0160].
  • A Commercial Broadcaster is a webcaster with a material business in FCC licensed AM or FM stations, so a non-broadcaster app is outside that subpart [M-0161].
  • Web VI other licensees: Educational Media Foundation $7,125,000 for 2026 [M-0163]; noncommercial educational minimum fee $800 in 2026 [M-0164].
  • NSS V (2026 to 2030) covers new subscription services delivered as part of a cable or satellite TV bundle [M-0166]. Stand-alone monthly per-subscriber base is $0.0234 (2025 rate, then adjusted) [M-0165]. Bundled base is $0.0390 [M-0507].
  • Preexisting subscription services deliver Reports of Use to the Collective within 45 days after each month [M-0178]. A Collective is the organization the Judges designate for a statutory licence [M-0305].

SoundExchange and the money (US federal)

  • SoundExchange says it is the sole organization the US government designated to administer the 114 licence [M-0294]. That is its own description, captured 2026-11-10.
  • It says it collects from services such as Pandora, SiriusXM and iHeartRadio [M-0295]. Naming a service is not a statement of its current licence status.
  • A nonprofit collective picked by the Judges distributes 114 receipts [M-0304]. Split: 50 percent to the sound recording copyright owner [M-0302], 45 percent to featured recording artists [M-0303].

112 ephemeral copies (US federal statute)

  • 112(a)(1) excludes motion pictures and other audiovisual works [M-0117].
  • 112(e)(1) gives a transmitting organization entitled to transmit under 114(d)(1)(C)(iv) or a 114(f) licence a statutory licence for no more than 1 ephemeral phonorecord, if conditions are met [M-0116].

Pending bills (not law)

  • AMFA: H.R. 861 would give sound recordings a public performance right by audio transmission, reaching terrestrial broadcast, and its audio transmission definition excludes audiovisual works [M-0480]. S. 326 was introduced 2025-01-30 and referred to Senate Judiciary [M-0504]. Neither is law.

Gotchas

  • Web V rates are 2025 only [M-0157, M-0307]. Web VI is not fully codified: none is in the saved sources for a commercial non-broadcaster as of 2026-11-10 [M-0010, M-0161] (unverified, M-0182). Do not state a Web VI rate for a commercial non-broadcaster. The Web VI rates above are for broadcasters and other named classes only.
  • NSS V 2026 amounts are adjusted bases. The saved text has placeholders for the 2026 figures [M-0165]. Do not quote a 2026 dollar amount.
  • "114 licence" does not mean a platform may host a song. It covers audio transmissions only [M-0112].
  • The sound recording has no general public performance right [M-0296]. Composition performance is a separate right, see M05 for PROs.
  • Statutory licence terms depend on listed conditions [M-0114]. Do not assume a service qualifies.
  • AMFA is a bill [M-0480, M-0504]. Do not say terrestrial radio pays sound recording owners.
  • SoundExchange sources here are self-description and may change [M-0294, M-0295].

Open questions for counsel

  • US federal: does any platform feature (audio only previews, live audio streams, playlists) fall within a 114(d)(2) noninteractive service, or is every product interactive or audiovisual?
  • US federal: for audio only live streams with background music, which licences are needed and who grants them?
  • US federal: does a platform's re-encoding of a user's audio create any reproduction or performance exposure for the platform as a service (M-0317 is unverified)?
  • US federal: what is the status of the Web VI commercial non-broadcaster determination (docket 24-CRB-0001-W)?
  • US federal: should the Web V and NSS V rate figures be rechecked before any rate quote, since the NSS V 2026 figures are adjusted amounts not in the saved text?
  • US federal: is there any reason to register with SoundExchange as a service at all?
