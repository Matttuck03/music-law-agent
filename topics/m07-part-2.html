M07 Sync and master use licensing for video

Part 2 of 3: Rules (continued). Law last checked 2026-11-10. Volatility: high.

Parts: 1 2 3

Rules (continued)

  • The licence reaches subscription transmissions, eligible nonsubscription transmissions and preexisting satellite DARS, and the transmission must not be part of an interactive service [M-0114, M-0115, M-0298]. An interactive service lets a member of the public receive on request a transmission of a particular sound recording [M-0113, M-0299]. Eligible nonsubscription means noninteractive digital audio programming [M-0300].
  • (analysis) The 114 licence does not cover short videos on a user upload platform, because they are audiovisual [M-0312, M-0180]. If such a platform were an interactive service, it would still need a licence for the public performance of each musical work in the recording [M-0301, M-0313].
  • Rate standard and period for the statutory licence are willing buyer and willing seller, set for five year periods [M-0167, M-0168]. Receipts go 50 percent to the recording owner, 45 percent to featured artists, via a designated nonprofit collective [M-0302, M-0303, M-0304]. This is background only. It does not license video.

Who grants sync and master use (industry practice)

  • Publishers act as owner or administrator of compositions and earn mechanical, performance, sync and other income [M-0079]. Sync is use of the composition combined with visual images [M-0081].
  • Each sync licence is generally negotiated with the prospective licensee, and publishers pay songwriters or heirs a contractual share [M-0080, M-0100]. These are company statements in SEC filings, not law.
  • Independent publishers license for film, television, advertising and digital platforms including social media and background music [M-0102].
  • Master side: labels license recordings under multi year, worldwide, non automatic deals with audit rights [M-0083, M-0084]. Streaming services also license independents and aggregators [M-0085].
  • Even a streaming service holding audio licences needed separate direct licences for audiovisual rights: Spotify says it recently signed direct US publisher deals for audiovisual and other rights [M-0087]. Audio-only US mechanicals run through 115 and the blanket licence [M-0086].
  • Public performance of compositions comes through PROs under blanket licences [M-0088]. A sync licence is not a performance licence, and PRO coverage is separate [M-0183].
