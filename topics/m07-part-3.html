M07 Sync and master use licensing for video

Part 3 of 3: Rules (continued), Gotchas, Open questions for counsel, Sources. Law last checked 2026-11-10. Volatility: high.

Parts: 1 2 3

Rules (continued)

  • Chain of title can be messy: label deals often treat artist services as work made for hire, with termination risk if a court disagreed [M-0052, M-0053, M-0099]. Artist deals are recoupable, option based, sometimes 360 [M-0075, M-0076, M-0077, M-0078].
  • Licensors in general include labels, aggregators, artists, publishers and PROs, and losing a licence is a stated business risk for services [M-0089].

Libraries and platform sound collections (platform terms)

Library and platform terms, as captured 2026-11-10. Terms change, so re-check before relying.

  • Epidemic Sound standard licence: personal use only, so corporate or third party content needs other licences [M-0404]; no sublicensing to third parties [M-0405].
  • Musicbed: licence is per single audiovisual Project, and excludes public performance where a PRO licence is available [M-0408].
  • Artlist: the saved text is site terms only; the music licence itself is not saved, so nothing is confirmed [M-0410].
  • YouTube Audio Library: described as copyright safe and not claimed through Content ID [M-0411]. Creative Commons tracks need credit in the description [M-0412]. YouTube disclaims responsibility for music from other libraries [M-0413].
  • TikTok Commercial Music Library (CML) User Terms: for Commercial Uses, Commercial Sounds may be posted only within TikTok [M-0382]. Posting on third party platforms needs separate rights holder licences [M-0383]. Sounds may not be used with content violating TikTok's terms or Community Guidelines [M-0384]. (analysis) This gives no rights for use on another platform [M-0386].
  • Meta Sound Collection (2022 terms, 2023 archive copy): limited to use on Meta products only [M-0385]. Current page not obtained.
  • Content ID is a matching system, not a licence. It scans uploads against owner files with outcomes block, monetize or track [M-0378]. It is for owners with exclusive rights to a substantial body of frequently uploaded material [M-0379].

Gotchas

  • "It is on the MLC" or "it is licensed for streaming" does not mean it is licensed for video. Mechanical audio is not sync [M-0340, M-0342].
  • Library licences are often per project, per licensee and non sublicensable. A creator licence may not pass to a platform or to a third party buyer [M-0404, M-0405, M-0408].
  • Platform sound licences are platform bound. TikTok commercial sounds stay in TikTok [M-0382, M-0383].
  • Content ID matching is not a clearance [M-0378].
  • Artlist music licence, TikTok Community Guidelines and the Meta Sound Collection current terms are needs source gaps. Meta's saved text is a 2023 archive [M-0385, M-0386, M-0410].
  • Statutory text is stable. Library and platform terms are volatile: they were captured 2026-11-10 and can change without notice [M-0386, M-0408, M-0410].
  • Filings (WMG, Spotify, Reservoir) are company statements about practice. They show how deals work, not what the law requires [M-0080, M-0087].

Open questions for counsel

  1. US federal: does placing a creator video re-encoded by a separate tool on a hosting platform create any separate licence need for the tool, or does exposure sit with the creator and the host [M-0317] (unverified)?
  2. US federal: what direct licence structure (sync plus master use, or a blanket publisher and label deal) fits a short video app with creator uploaded music, and will majors license that use at all?
  3. US federal: do live streams with music need a different set of composition and master licences than recorded uploads?
  4. What notice and takedown posture should a platform take for music in creator videos, given licences are missing (see M08)?
