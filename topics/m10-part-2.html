M10 Content ID systems and platform sound libraries

Part 2 of 2: Rules (continued), Gotchas, Open questions for counsel, Sources. Law last checked 2026-11-10. Volatility: high.

Parts: 1 2

Rules (continued)

  • Without authorisation the provider is liable unless it made best efforts to get one [M-0389], best efforts to high industry standards to make identified works unavailable [M-0390], and acted expeditiously on notice and made best efforts to prevent re-upload [M-0391].
  • Providers under three years old with turnover under EUR 10 million face a lighter regime [M-0392].
  • Users may rely on quotation, criticism, review, caricature, parody or pastiche [M-0393]. There is no general monitoring duty [M-0394]. Complaints get human review of disable or remove decisions [M-0395].
  • CJEU Poland v Parliament (C-401/19, 26 April 2022) upheld Article 17 [M-0396]. Providers cannot be required to filter content whose unlawfulness needs an independent assessment of context and exceptions [M-0397].
  • Commission 2021 guidance (non-binding) says automated blocking at upload should in principle cover only manifestly infringing uploads [M-0398].
  • Text and data mining exceptions (CDSM Arts 3 and 4) are an AI topic, not a Content ID rule: Art 3 for research bodies [M-0399], Art 4 unless rightholders reserved use in machine readable form [M-0400, M-0442]. National transposition varies [M-0442].

Platform sound libraries and library licences (platform terms)

  • TikTok Commercial Music Library: a user engaging in Commercial Uses may post videos with Commercial Sounds only within TikTok [M-0382]. Posting on third party platforms needs separate rights-holder licences [M-0383]. Sounds may not be used with content that breaks TikTok's Terms or Community Guidelines [M-0384]. Edition 2026-11-10.
  • Meta Sound Collection: licence limited to Meta products [M-0385]. This is a 2022 terms copy archived 2023-08-18. The current copy was not obtained.
  • Epidemic Sound: the personal licence is for the licensee's own personal use [M-0404] and does not allow sublicensing [M-0405]. Edition SingleTrackLicensesV8.
  • Musicbed: the licence is per single audiovisual project and excludes public performance where a PRO licence is available [M-0408].
  • Artlist: the saved text is only the site terms of use, and the music licence is not in it [M-0410].

Gotchas

  • Absence findings (Artlist site terms, YouTube Audio Library page) mean nothing was found in one saved page [M-0410, M-0411]. They do not prove permission.
  • Gap, needs source: TikTok Community Guidelines are unchecked, and the current Meta Sound Collection terms were not obtained [M-0385, M-0384]. The Artlist music licence is not saved [M-0410].
  • Library terms change. All editions here were captured 2026-11-10 except Meta (2023-08-18) and Pond5 (archive capture, October 2026). Recheck before relying.
  • Content ID is not a licence. A claim or monetize outcome is a platform rule between owner and platform, not a grant to any other platform [M-0378] (analysis).
  • A takedown sender who ignores fair use can face 512(f) damages in the Ninth Circuit [M-0360, M-0359], but a counter notice sent in bad faith carries perjury and misrepresentation risk too [M-0356, M-0359].
  • Copyright Office report views are not law [M-0373, M-0376]. Commission guidance is non-binding [M-0398].
  • 512(c)(1)(B) has a contested case law gloss on "right and ability to control" not covered by the ledger [M-0347].

Open questions for counsel

  • US: does a platform's live streaming fit 512(c) storage at a user's direction, or is it outside the safe harbor?
  • US: is an in-app sound picker backed by a library licence a direct licence to the platform, or only to the creator, and do the use limits in that licence bind?
  • US: does a re-encode or audio processing tool qualify as a service provider under 512, or does it face direct liability for copying audio?
  • US and EU: should a platform adopt audio fingerprinting now, and if it does, does acting on matches create knowledge that cuts against the safe harbor?
  • EU: is a user upload platform an online content sharing service provider under Article 17, and does the three year startup tier apply [M-0392]?
