M13 Work for hire, transfers, termination

Part 3 of 3: Gotchas, Open questions for counsel, Sources. Law last checked 2026-11-10. Volatility: medium.

Parts: 1 2 3

Gotchas

  • A "work for hire" label in a contract is not self proving. Statute needs employee status or a listed category plus a signed writing [M-0045, M-0046].
  • Recordings: the categories do not name sound recordings, and no case in this card decides the point [M-0047, M-0049].
  • Termination cannot be contracted away [M-0059]. Work made for hire is outside it [M-0055, M-0063], so the work for hire fight is also the termination fight [M-0070].
  • Termination timing: Section 203 grants made from 1978 open their window 35 years later, so the earliest windows are already open (analysis, arithmetic from [M-0056]). The 304(c) window uses 56 years [M-0064].
  • Termination needs recorded notice. An unrecorded notice does not take effect [M-0058].
  • Mills Music (1985) still stands in the saved record, but the 2024 Office rule bars the exception for the MLC blanket licence [M-0067, M-0071]. Do not state the 2024 rule as limiting voluntary licences without the MLC direction clause [M-0072].
  • Ray Charles decided standing, not whether the works were for hire [M-0069].
  • Waite (S.D.N.Y.), Marvel (2d Cir.) and Ray Charles (9th Cir.) are lower court decisions, not Supreme Court rulings, and bind only their own court or circuit. Other courts may differ (analysis).
  • Waite is a motion to dismiss ruling (31 March 2020). It decided nothing on the merits of work for hire [M-0049]. Later history is not in the ledger.
  • Marvel v. Kirby: the quote is PARTIAL, and later history of the judgment against two defendants is not in the saved source [M-0054].
  • M-0032 is an inference about the audio of a short video and stays unverified. Say so in any answer.
  • Not in the ledger: foreign termination or work for hire rules (UK and EU are other cards), state law on assignments, the Estate of Smith (2d Cir. 2020) text, any Grande or later history. Say "not checked", never guess.

Open questions for counsel

  • US: does this creator agreement (attach it) make a commissioned recording a work made for hire, or is a separate written assignment needed?
  • US: for this track, what is the chain of title for the composition and the recording, and can the grantor sign a sync and master licence on the grant date?
  • US: has a Section 203 or 304(c) notice been served or recorded against this catalogue, and does the derivative works exception reach new videos?
  • US: does a platform's creator licence, as written, cover music a creator claims to own, and what warranty and indemnity should it contain?
  • US: are a platform's terms of use enough where a user uploads audio they do not own?
  • US: after the 2024 Office rule, how should a licensor and payee be identified where an MLC blanket licence and a terminated grant overlap?
