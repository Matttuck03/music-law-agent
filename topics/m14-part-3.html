M14 Deal terms

Part 3 of 3: Rules (continued), Gotchas, Open questions for counsel, Sources. Law last checked 2026-11-10. Volatility: medium.

Parts: 1 2 3

Rules (continued)

  • The author is first owner [M-0090]. An employee's musical work or film belongs first to the employer, subject to contrary agreement [M-0091]. The author of a sound recording is the producer [M-0092]. So (analysis) a UK sound recording's first owner is the producer, as the employer rule does not list sound recordings [M-0093].
  • An assignment must be in writing signed by or for the assignor [M-0096]. An exclusive licence must be in writing signed by or for the owner [M-0097]. An agreement to assign future copyright, signed by the prospective owner, takes effect as an assignment when the copyright arises [M-0098].
  • The owner has exclusive rights to copy, issue, rent, lend, perform, communicate to the public and adapt [M-0234]. Infringement includes authorising another to do a restricted act [M-0235]. Communication to the public covers musical works and recordings [M-0236], including on demand availability [M-0237]. (Analysis) a UK facing platform may face direct or authorising liability [M-0255].

Digital replicas in deals (California, statute)

  • Digital replica means a computer generated, highly realistic representation readily identifiable as a person's voice or visual likeness [M-0447].
  • Producing or making available a deceased personality's digital replica in an expressive audiovisual work or sound recording without consent carries the greater of $10,000 or actual damages, subject to listed exceptions [M-0448].
  • A contract term letting a digital replica replace in person work is unenforceable for new performances from 1 January 2025 if it lacks a reasonably specific description of uses and the individual had no representation [M-0449].

Gotchas

  • WMG and Reservoir lines describe each company's contracts. Courts have not all agreed that recordings are works made for hire (analysis from M-0049, M-0051). Waite is a motion to dismiss ruling only.
  • WMG says the window is 35 years from release; the statute counts from execution, or publication for grants covering it [M-0053, M-0056].
  • Termination cannot be waived by contract [M-0059], so a deal clause saying otherwise does not stop it.
  • Filings are dated: WMG fiscal year ended 30 September 2025, Spotify year ended 31 December 2025, Reservoir year ended 31 March 2026. Terms and licence lengths change.
  • The Spotify direct publisher AV deals are narrow: some US publishers only [M-0087]. Do not read them as a general video licence.
  • The M-0086 statutory route is audio only. A mechanical licence is not sync.
  • California digital replica rows are statutes as captured 2026-11-10. California Civil Code 3344.1 covers deceased personalities, not living ones [M-0448].
  • UK rules above are English law under the CDPA, not a UK guide to platform liability. UK platform case law is not in the ledger [M-0255].
  • Blurring, muting or low audio quality is not a licence, and "the creator said it was theirs" is not a signed writing.

Open questions for counsel

  • US: does an uploader's licence from a distributor or aggregator ever include sync or master use for short video, or only audio distribution?
  • US: if a creator's deal is a 360 or expanded rights deal, can the label claim a share of platform earnings from a video using the label's master?
  • US: what warranty and indemnity should a platform require from creators who say they hold sync and master rights?
  • US: does a platform's re-encoding of creator audio count as a covered use under common creator side licences, or does it need its own terms?
  • US: for a catalogue song where the grant was terminated, who now grants sync, and how does a platform video made before termination continue [M-0060]?
  • UK: does a platform need a UK licence structure if UK users can stream uploads, and who is the first owner of a creator made recording?
  • California: do a platform's creator terms need a reasonably specific description of uses to survive Lab. Code 927 for voice or likeness [M-0449]?
  • What currency check is due on the open gaps: Artlist licence terms, TikTok Community Guidelines and current Meta Sound Collection terms?
