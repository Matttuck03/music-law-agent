M15 AI training and outputs

Part 2 of 3: Rules (continued). Law last checked 2026-11-10. Volatility: high.

Parts: 1 2 3

Rules (continued)

  • CDSM Art. 3: text and data mining exception for research organisations and cultural heritage institutions, for scientific research [M-0399].
  • CDSM Art. 4: general mining exception for lawfully accessible works, unless the rightholder has reserved use in an appropriate manner [M-0400]. Art. 4(3) limits it to uses not expressly reserved, for online content by machine readable means [M-0442]. National transposition varies.
  • AI Act Art. 53(1)(c): providers of general purpose AI models need a copyright policy that identifies and honours Art. 4(3) reservations [M-0439]. Art. 53(1)(d): they must publish a sufficiently detailed training content summary on the AI Office template [M-0440]. These duties apply from 2 August 2025 [M-0441]. Regulation (EU) 2026/1744 was read as not amending Art. 53 or 113(b) per the ledger notes, but that regulation text is not saved (needs source).
  • The EU GPAI Code of Practice copyright chapter (voluntary, binds signatories only): Measure 1.3(1) commits signatories to comply with machine readable rights reservations when crawling [M-0493]. Measure 1.4(1)(a) commits them to technical safeguards against infringing reproduction in outputs [M-0494].
  • GEMA v. OpenAI (foreign court decision, LG Munich I, 11 Nov 2025, 42 O 14139/24, first instance, lyrics not audio): memorising works in a language model is a reproduction [M-0431]. The mining exception covers copies made to build training data but not training the model itself [M-0432]. Model operators, not users, were liable for infringing outputs from simple prompts [M-0433]. Appeal to OLG Munich pending per the source header; later status unchecked.
  • CDSM Art. 17 makes an online content sharing service responsible for user uploads and removes the hosting safe harbour for it [M-0387, M-0388]. See M08 and M19 for the upload regime.

UK

  • CDPA s.29A allows copies for text and data analysis only for non commercial research by a person with lawful access, with acknowledgement [M-0239]. It does not cover commercial training.
  • Data (Use and Access) Act 2025 s.135 required an economic impact assessment and s.136 a report, each within nine months of the Act [M-0434, M-0435].
  • The s.136 report (March 2026, policy statement not law) says a broad exception with opt out is no longer the preferred option [M-0436, M-0015]. No AI training exception is enacted [M-0436].
  • The report proposes removing protection for wholly computer generated works (CDPA s.9(3) type) while keeping protection for AI assisted works [M-0437, M-0016]. Proposal only.
  • It says a digital replica or personality right is under further consideration. Exploration only [M-0438]. See M16.
  • CDPA s.30A fair dealing for caricature, parody or pastiche exists, and a contract term restricting a permitted act is unenforceable [M-0240, M-0241].

  9. [M-0431] VERIFIED. GEMA v. OpenAI (LG Munich I, Nov. 11, 2025, 42 O 14139/24): memorising works in a language model is a reproduction under UrhG s.16. Translation: memorisation of language works in the AI model is a reproduction because the work is physically fixed and can be made indirectly perceptible.

    Source: LG München I, Urteil vom 11.11.2025, 42 O 14139/24, GEMA v OpenAI (Bayern.Recht official text, German). Pinpoint: Leitsatz 2 (Rn. 181). Jurisdiction: case-law. Checked 2026-11-10.

    Quote: "Die Memorisierung von Sprachwerken im KI-Sprachmodell stellt eine Vervielfältigung nach § 16 UrhG dar, weil das Werk körperlich festgelegt ist und es mittelbar wahrnehmbar gemacht werden kann."

  10. [M-0432] VERIFIED. GEMA v. OpenAI: the TDM exception (UrhG s.44b) covers copies made to build training data but not the training of the model itself. Translation: the barrier of s.44b covers reproduction when creating training data material, but not training the model.

    Source: LG München I, Urteil vom 11.11.2025, 42 O 14139/24, GEMA v OpenAI (Bayern.Recht official text, German). Pinpoint: Leitsatz 4 (Rn. 193). Jurisdiction: case-law. Checked 2026-11-10.

    Quote: "Unter die Schranke des § 44b UrhG unterfallen Vervielfältigung beim Erstellen des Trainigsdatenmaterials, nicht aber bei dem Training des Modells"

  11. [M-0433] VERIFIED. GEMA v. OpenAI: the model operators, not the users, are liable for infringing outputs produced by simple prompts. Translation: operators of the language model are liable for infringement through outputs because they control the act.

    Source: LG München I, Urteil vom 11.11.2025, 42 O 14139/24, GEMA v OpenAI (Bayern.Recht official text, German). Pinpoint: Leitsatz 5 (Rn. 275-277). Jurisdiction: case-law. Checked 2026-11-10.
