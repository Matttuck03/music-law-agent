M18 UK music copyright

Part 2 of 2: Rules (continued), Gotchas, Open questions for counsel, Sources. Law last checked 2026-11-10. Volatility: high.

Parts: 1 2

Rules (continued)

  • The report proposes removing protection for wholly computer generated works (the s.9(3) type) while keeping it for works made with AI assistance [M-0437, M-0016]. Proposal only.
  • The government is considering whether to introduce a new digital replica or personality right [M-0438]. Exploration only, not law.

Market context (UK, regulator report)

  • The CMA music streaming report records an argument that, because about 90% of Content ID claims are monetised rather than taken down, rightsholders do not see YouTube streams as cannibalising streaming revenue [M-0381]. That is an argument put to the CMA, not a CMA finding.

Gotchas

  • The UK has no fair use. Fair dealing exceptions are specific and narrow [M-0240, M-0239].
  • "Producer" is the author of a recording, which can differ from the artist or the label [M-0092, M-0093].
  • An assignment or exclusive licence not in signed writing fails the statute test [M-0096, M-0097].
  • The 2026 AI report is a policy statement. Do not say the UK allows or bans AI training on music on this basis [M-0436].
  • Cases on secondary liability from the US (BMG v. Cox 2018 and Cox v. Sony 2026) do not apply in the UK. See M09 for the US position.
  • Currency, as of 2026-11-10: the March 2026 AI report and its proposals can change at any time. Volatility is high. Check the legislation.gov.uk text before relying on any section, since amendments may follow.
  • Not in our sources: UK collecting society licence terms, UK case law on platform liability, UK moral rights, and UK term of protection. Needs source.

Open questions for counsel

  • UK: which societies or direct licences does a UK facing short video app need for composition and for recording?
  • UK: is a platform liable for authorising infringement when creators upload videos with unlicensed music, and does a UK notice and takedown practice reduce that risk?
  • UK: does a live stream playing a commercial recording need separate performance licences, and who pays performers' equitable remuneration?
  • UK: does a platform's server side re-encoding of user audio make it a direct infringer?
  • UK: can the parody or pastiche exception ever cover a short video using part of a track, and what are the limits?
  • UK: what is the current status of the AI copyright and digital replica proposals after the March 2026 report?
