M24 Licensed platform models, distributor rules and stem model licences

Part 1 of 2: Bottom line, Rules. Law last checked 2026-11-13. Volatility: high.

Answers questions like: How do licensed mix platforms clear the music; Will a distributor take a remix or a cover; Can we use an open source stem splitter in a paid product; Is a code licence the same as a licence for the model weights; What does it mean when a model page says nothing about a licence; Which licences would a mix platform need to sign

Parts: 1 2

Bottom line

  • The mix services in the saved set that describe themselves as licensed say they run on licences for both the songs and the recordings of every track, backed by direct deals with labels (company statements, captured 2026-11-13) [M-0586, M-0589, M-0593]. No saved source shows a mix platform running on commercial tracks without such deals.
  • Distributors treat a cover and a remix differently. The saved pages offer a route for covers and say sample-based or derivative works cannot use it [M-0596, M-0597, M-0599].
  • A stem splitter has two licences to check: one for the code and one for the trained model weights. An MIT licence on the code is not a licence for the weights [M-0600, M-0603]. Where a project says nothing about its weights, that is silence, not a grant [M-0604, M-0607].
  • Of the stem models checked on 2026-11-13, none has weights with a stated licence that clearly allows commercial use. One is stated non-commercial [M-0606]. One maintainer says the weights are for personal or research use [M-0603]. The rest are silent [M-0604, M-0607, M-0609, M-0610].

Rules

  • Mixcloud (platform terms: company blog, not terms of service and not law; blog dated 2024-03-28, page path after redirect dated 2025-01-01, captured 2026-11-13): a streaming service must hold licences that clear both the composition and the sound recording of every track in a stream [M-0586]. It says it was the first streaming service to clear the copyrights for radio shows and mixes [M-0587].
  • Mixcloud (same blog, same capture date): its checklist for choosing a platform asks whether the platform has direct deals with the labels whose music is played [M-0589]. It says downloading needs an additional licensed right, which it calls the mechanical right [M-0588]. That is the company's short version, not a statement of US law.
  • Mixcloud (same blog, same capture date): it describes its live product as a licensed live streaming platform that also archives the stream [M-0590]. On a platform that has not cleared the music, rights holders can have a mix taken down or muted [M-0591].
  • Apple Music (platform terms: company notice to artists dated 2021-11-16, captured 2026-11-13): it says it built a process to identify and compensate the individual creators in DJ mixes [M-0592]. It says it works with major and independent labels and pays rights holders within DJ mixes directly [M-0593].
  • A large on-demand service's public filing (industry practice, Spotify annual report for fiscal year 2025): in the US, mechanical rights for audio-only use run through the section 115 compulsory licence, with a blanket licence run by the Mechanical Licensing Collective [M-0086]. Public performance rights for compositions come through the performing rights organisations ASCAP, BMI, GMR and SESAC under blanket licences [M-0088].
  • US federal, 17 U.S.C. 114: the statutory licence for recordings is closed to interactive services [M-0115] and to archived programs under 5 hours [M-0578]. An interactive service also needs a licence for each song [M-0301]. So an on-demand mix library has to get its master rights by direct deal (analysis).
  • US federal (court order, ASCAP consent decree): ASCAP may hold and license only non-exclusive public performance rights [M-0256]. It cannot grant sync rights (analysis row) [M-0311]. The decrees bind only ASCAP and BMI [M-0278]. An ASCAP licence therefore does not cover copying, remixing or video (analysis) [M-0183]. The base has no saved text on the scope of the other organisations' licences.
  • CD Baby (platform terms, help page, web archive copy dated 2026-02-16, captured 2026-11-13): a cover release needs a mechanical licence for digital downloads. That licence covers the composition, not the original recording [M-0596]. Derivative works such as samples cannot be cleared through its cover song process [M-0597]. Even a streaming-only release of a cover needs at least a digital download licence under its rules [M-0598].
  • CD Baby (platform terms, artist education article, live page, undated, captured 2026-11-13): using a sample of a recording means clearing the master with the label and the composition with the publisher. There is no standard rate, and the deal is negotiated directly [M-0599].
  • TuneCore (platform terms, help page, web archive copy dated 2020-05-07, captured 2026-11-13, possibly stale): a cover may need permission and possibly a licence from the original copyright holder [M-0594]. Some stores did not accept soundalike covers, and content hidden by a store was not reversed or refunded [M-0595].
  • Demucs (industry, checked 2026-11-13): the code licence file is MIT and says nothing about model weights [M-0600]. The project README says the licence of the weights is "not clear" because the training set includes research-only data [M-0601]. A maintainer says he cannot grant a licence for the weights [M-0602]. A maintainer comment dated 2022-09-29 says the code is free for commercial use under MIT, while the weights are research artefacts for personal or research usage [M-0603].
  • Spleeter (industry, checked 2026-11-13): the code is MIT. The README is silent on a licence for the pretrained models: silence, not a grant [M-0604]. Its disclaimer tells users to get authorisation from right owners before using it on copyrighted material [M-0605].
  • Open-Unmix (industry, checked 2026-11-13): the umxl weights are licensed CC BY-NC-SA 4.0, which is non-commercial [M-0606]. The umx and umxhq models were trained on the MUSDB18-HQ set, and no weights licence is stated for them: silence, not a grant [M-0607].
