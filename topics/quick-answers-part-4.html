The tool and the platform (continued)

  • Statutory damages run $750 to $30,000 per work [M-0215], up to $150,000 per work if willful [M-0217], counted per work, not per view (analysis row) [M-0254]. They depend on timely registration [M-0214].

Stem models

32. The stem model's code is MIT. Can we ship it in a paid app?

  • The code, yes on its face. The weights are a separate question. For Demucs the licence file is silent on weights [M-0600] and a maintainer says the weights are for personal or research usage [M-0603]. Checked 2026-11-13.

33. The model page says nothing about a licence. Is that permission?

  • No. Silence is not a grant [M-0604, M-0607, M-0609]. Get a written grant from someone able to give it.

34. Which of the checked models are clearly fine for commercial use?

  • None, on their stated weights terms as checked 2026-11-13. One is non-commercial [M-0606]. One is described as research or personal use [M-0603]. The others are silent [M-0604, M-0609, M-0610].

Other countries

35. Does Canada's user content exception make mixes legal there?

  • It can protect an individual, not clearly a platform. Conditions: solely non-commercial [M-0616], source named where reasonable [M-0617], reasonable belief the source copy was lawful [M-0618], and no substantial harm to the market for the original [M-0619]. The platform's own position rests on inference [M-0620].

36. Do the UK and EU pastiche rules cover mixes?

  • Not as a general rule. The UK has a fair dealing exception for caricature, parody or pastiche [M-0240]. The EU court's 2026 press release reads pastiche to include some sampling in artistic dialogue [M-0631], and says it is not a catch-all [M-0633]. That row rests on a press release, not the judgment.

37. Is the platform itself liable for uploads in the EU?

  • Yes, if the service counts as an online content-sharing service provider, unless it is licensed or meets the best efforts tests. A content-sharing platform communicates user uploads to the public and must get authorisation [M-0387, M-0389]. New, small providers have a lighter regime [M-0392].

38. What about Australia?

  • The saved sections cover parody or satire [M-0622, M-0623] and criticism or review with acknowledgement [M-0625]. Authors also have an integrity right [M-0626]. The base holds no Australian user content exception and makes no claim about the rest of the Act.
